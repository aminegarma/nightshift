**Correction to the reconcile file.** The reconcile file was written *before* the last review decisions of 2026-11-02. It says to remove the dangerous-repair approvals and the recovery-plan review. On a platform-only repair, an authorised Orvex engineer approves and the plan is reviewed. So rows 1 and 2 below are **kept, re-scoped**, not removed.

## Also stale, found while re-checking (not in the reconcile count)

- **B3 after Reject:** "It won't ask for this repair again on its own" is now incomplete. FR-50: if the problem becomes critical or materially changes, Nightshift may send **one** new request, which shows the earlier rejection. A later recurrence is a new problem. The last-resort condition can't override a rejection.
