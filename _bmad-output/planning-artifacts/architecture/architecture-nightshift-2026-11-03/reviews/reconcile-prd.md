# Reconcile: ARCHITECTURE-SPINE.md against PRD (final 2026-11-03) and addendum "For the architecture"

Reviewed 2026-11-11. Read-only review. Inputs: `ARCHITECTURE-SPINE.md` (updated 2026-11-11), `prds/prd-nightshift-2026-10-28/prd.md`, `addendum.md` (For the architecture, plus the security/architecture hand-offs it points to), `.working/prd-change-queue.md` (AP-1..AP-7), `.memlog.md` (authority on decisions).

Rule used: a conflict covered by AP-1..AP-7 is "pending PRD change", not a miss. Severity: **critical** = the spine contradicts a must-requirement or would let builders violate privacy or safety; **high** = a requirement with real architectural impact is missing; **medium** = partly covered or weakened; **low** = wording, traceability or small gaps.

Counts: **critical 2 · high 15 · medium 10 · low 8**.
