# Polish of the 2026-11-02 update: PRD Nightshift

**Read of purpose and audience:** this document exists to help Orvex's judge (a technical founder) assess what Nightshift must do and why, and to give the UX and architecture authors an unambiguous set of requirements, terms and constraints to design against. The 2026-11-02 update adds Danny's Exchange 5 (approval by what a repair changes, the agreed management policy, manual dangerous repairs, external-AI consent, outbound channels) and the changes queued from the UX work.

**Scope:** only text changed or added on 2026-11-02 (`diff prd-final-2026-10-31.md prd.md`, about 5,900 words on changed lines). Text polished on 2026-10-31 is touched only where a changed passage now duplicates or clashes with it.

**Passes run:** structure, then prose on top of the structure findings. Structure model: Strategic/Context (Pyramid), the model for PRDs. The Glossary is judged as Reference/Database (definition first, one home per rule, "See FR-x" pointers).

**Style and voice to preserve:** plain, short declarative sentences for a non-expert reader and a technical founder judge; British spelling; no serial comma; bracketed source tags on statements, such as "(Amine, 2026-11-02, after the review of the update)" or "(review fix, 2026-11-02, approved by Amine)"; "tick/ticked" for advance permission; "[OPEN]", "[To test]" and "[DESIGN ASSUMPTION ...]" markers; the canonical terms set on 2026-10-31 (Orvex technical approval, advance permission vs approval, approval request, problem, on duty). No row below changes a rule, decision, number, actor or condition. Uncertain fixes are phrased "Consider: ...?"; QUESTION rows are for the author to decide.

**How to apply:** each Original Text is an exact, unique substring of `prd.md`. Where one row covers several places, the pieces are separated by " ‖ " and the Revised Text pieces follow the same order. In Revised Text, `<br>` marks a line break. Rows are written so that no two rows edit the same substring.

## Summary

- **Recommendations:** 22 structure (9 MOVE, of which 5 reorder bullets or sentences and 1 also condenses; 1 MERGE; 2 CUT; 5 CONDENSE; 3 QUESTION; 2 PRESERVE) and 18 prose.
- **Estimated reduction if all structure rows are accepted:** about 145 words (including the QUESTION cut in 3.2). That is about 2.5% of the roughly 5,900 words on changed lines, or 1.0% of the PRD's 14,950 words. The prose rows are about word-neutral. No length target was given.
- **Biggest wins for comprehension:** FR-52 moved into 5.5 next to the approval rules it depends on; FR-21's and FR-51's bullets reordered so scope and limits come first; four verbatim Glossary/FR duplicates reduced to one home each (tick scope, incident history, Slack-only approval, PRIV-9/FR-6 heartbeat consequences).
- **Comprehension trade-offs:** cutting the Glossary's tick-scope and incident-history sentences means a Glossary-only reader must follow the pointer to FR-21 or FR-37. Condensing FR-52's tags puts provenance in a block tag that is read once. Both are deliberate; the Alert-only and Agreed management policy entries are preserved as reinforcement.
- **Term inconsistencies in the new text:** "Orvex's approval" / "Orvex approval" vs Orvex technical approval; "executor" as a person (FR-52) vs the SEC-8 component; "mode" for working without AI; "hosted model" where external AI is meant (PRIV-2); "accepting the AI's draft" vs "AI draft, accepted by Amine".
