Sections tied to a review ("Input for the privacy and security review and the architecture" from 2026-10-31, "From the review of the 2026-11-02 update") hold open design questions handed on.

**Answer to the placement question:** "For the UX" is well placed. It is a sibling of "For the architecture" (settled inputs for one deliverable) and belongs next to it, before the review hand-offs. Moving it after the review sections would split the settled inputs. The new review section fits the pattern of the 2026-10-31 one (dated review findings handed on instead of added to the PRD). Its provenance-based heading suits it, because it serves four deliverables. It does not duplicate the earlier sections word for word, but every item in it extends an earlier item without saying so. The fix is cross-references (rows 2 to 5), not a merge: merging would rewrite the polished 2026-10-31 text and lose the per-review trail.

**Voice and style to preserve:** terse, note-like register; bold lead-in labels; fragments ("Design details from…"); semicolon-joined lists inside a bullet; bracketed source tags and finding IDs; dated provenance in headings and parentheses. British spelling and no serial comma are author directives.
