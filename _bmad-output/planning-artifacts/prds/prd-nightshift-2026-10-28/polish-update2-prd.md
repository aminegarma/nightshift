# Polish of the 2026-11-03 update: PRD Nightshift

**Read of purpose and audience:** this document exists to help Orvex's judge (a technical founder) assess what Nightshift must do and why, and to give the UX and architecture authors an unambiguous set of requirements, terms and constraints to design against. The 2026-11-03 update adds the decisions from the UX work and the review of the update: the required Fleet heartbeat, approval validity, withdrawal and revocation, inconclusive pre-run checks, group approval, management-policy changes and onboarding defaults.

**Scope:** only text changed or added on 2026-11-03 (`diff prd-before-update-2026-11-03.md prd.md`, about 3,250 words on changed lines), with enough context to judge them. Frontmatter, the status line and the memlog are not touched.

**Passes run:** structure, then prose on top of the structure findings. Structure model: Strategic/Context (Pyramid), the model for PRDs; the Glossary is judged as Reference (definition first, one home per rule, "See FR-x" pointers).

**Style and voice to preserve:** plain, short declarative sentences; British spelling; no serial comma; bracketed source tags; the terms "pre-run check", "node heartbeat", "Fleet heartbeat", "service settings", "no less restrictive" and "not offered in the first version"; "[OPEN]" markers. No row changes a requirement, ID, quoted string, tag or attribution.

**Status:** every row marked "Applied" was applied to `prd.md` on 2026-11-03. Rows marked "Skipped" need a decision from Amine.
